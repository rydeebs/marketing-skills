# Builder / GC relationships

## Purpose and inputs

Research builders and general contractors and prepare a subcontractor or specialist relationship pipeline. Adapt to the user’s actual business; do not assume West Coast Deck is the client.

Start with: Target project geography, trade scope, capacity, verified qualifications, portfolio, ideal builder profile, and current relationship records. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Identify custom builders and remodelers whose published work fits the business’s specialty. Separate actual decision makers from unverified directory contacts.

2. Record fit evidence, likely collaboration need, geography, contact source, and relationship owner. Check for duplicate organizations and existing opportunities.

3. Prepare a concise introduction with a relevant project, clearly bounded capabilities, and a proposed next step. Request operational confirmation for capacity, insurance, licensing, or lead-time claims.

4. Build a manageable follow-up queue and track responses, meetings, bid invitations, awarded work, and reasons for poor fit. Stop outreach on refusal or opt-out.

## Deliverable

Return a sourced builder pipeline, portfolio selections, communication drafts, capability gaps needing confirmation, and next-action dates.

## Quality checks

Do not equate an invitation to bid with revenue. Preserve the distinction between relationship development and a binding quote, contract, or performance commitment.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
