# Google Business Profile

## Purpose and inputs

Maintain a local service business Google Business Profile and prepare project posts, photo updates, factual answers, and review responses. Adapt to the user’s actual business; do not assume West Coast Deck is the client.

Start with: Business profile URL, verified services and service area, current hours, approved project photos, booking link, and recent reviews or questions. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Compare the live profile with the business facts supplied by the owner. List inaccurate hours, service details, links, and coverage areas before proposing changes; never invent an office location.

2. Build a weekly queue of two to four useful posts when enough real material exists. Give each a specific project or homeowner question, one relevant image, and a clear next step. Avoid recycling the same promotion.

3. Select fresh project images and write accurate service descriptions. Remove private addresses, faces, or identifying details when sharing permission is absent.

4. Read new questions and reviews. Draft specific answers from verified facts, route unresolved customer issues to the owner, and confirm which profile features are currently available before attempting updates.

## Deliverable

Deliver a dated profile change log and content queue with post text, asset, destination link, planned date, and status. Include review/question drafts and an owner-needed list.

## Quality checks

Every claim must be supported by business records. Distinguish drafts, scheduled work, and verified live changes; measure inquiries and bookings separately from profile views.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
