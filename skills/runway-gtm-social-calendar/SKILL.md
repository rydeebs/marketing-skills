# Social calendar

## Purpose and inputs

Build and maintain Runway’s organic social calendar across X, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and YouTube, including captions, creator reposts, and response drafts.

Runway (runwayml.com) builds generative video and media models and tools, sold through a self-serve web app, team and enterprise plans, and a developer API. Buyers range from individual creators and creative professionals to agencies, brand marketing teams, media and entertainment studios, gaming companies, and developers building on the API. Check current model names, features, plan tiers, pricing, usage terms, and content policy against runwayml.com, the Help Center, or internal docs before stating them. Do not rely on memory, because the lineup changes often.

Start with: Brand voice, channel strategy, approved generations and creator work (with permissions), launch calendar, account destinations, publishing cadence, and current comments and DMs. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Group usable assets by theme: new capabilities, creator showcases, tutorials and workflows, customer stories, research, and events. Credit creators and confirm repost permission.

2. Prepare a balanced calendar across launches, education, community work, and proof. Choose the format (thread, carousel, reel or short, LinkedIn post, livestream) based on the asset and audience.

3. Write channel-specific captions with a strong hook, accurate detail about how the work was made (models, tools, human editing), and a clear CTA. Tag creators and partners only when accurate and approved.

4. Draft replies to routine questions. Route support issues, sales interest, press inquiries, and controversy to the right owner.

## Deliverable

Deliver a calendar with channel, date, format, asset links, caption, credits, CTA, and publication status, plus a response queue with priority and owner.

## Quality checks

Check asset rights, creator credit, AI disclosure, crops, and links. Report published URLs and downstream signups or engagement from the right audience, not only likes. Never overstate model capabilities or show cherry-picked generations as typical output without saying so. Respect likeness, voice, and IP rights in any generated or customer media, follow Runway’s content policy and AI-disclosure norms, and keep NDA’d customer, studio, and partner names out of public material without written approval.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
