# Jobsite marketing

## Purpose and inputs

Coordinate signs, banners, and nearby marketing opportunities around active home-service jobs. Adapt to the user’s actual business; do not assume West Coast Deck is the client.

Start with: Active-project schedule, customer permissions, site restrictions, field contacts, available signage, and marketing inventory. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Create a site roster with project stage, field owner, approved public location detail, and eligibility for signage or surrounding outreach.

2. Confirm customer consent and relevant HOA, property, and local placement restrictions. Define what materials can be placed, where, and for how long.

3. Prepare a field checklist for delivery, safe placement, proof photo, condition checks, and removal. Assign a person and due date to each physical action.

4. Identify relevant nearby neighborhoods for a separate mailer or permitted door-hanger plan. Connect inquiries to the project campaign using an approved tracking method.

## Deliverable

Deliver a site marketing roster, material/inventory needs, field assignments, evidence checklist, and installation/removal status.

## Quality checks

A remote agent cannot confirm a sign is physically deployed without field evidence. Do not expose residential addresses publicly or authorize entry onto private property.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
