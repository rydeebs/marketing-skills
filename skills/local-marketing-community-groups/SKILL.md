# Facebook Groups / Nextdoor

## Purpose and inputs

Monitor relevant community groups for homeowner questions and prepare helpful, rule-compliant responses and opportunity handoffs. Adapt to the user’s actual business; do not assume West Coast Deck is the client.

Start with: Service area, permitted account identity, group access, community rules, verified capabilities, and approved project/educational content. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Catalog relevant communities with geography, audience, promotion rules, allowed days, and membership/access limitations. Read current rules before drafting a promotional response.

2. Identify specific recommendation requests and genuine questions where the business can help. Record the post link, date, need, fit, and response deadline.

3. Draft a direct, useful response that discloses the business relationship. Answer the actual question, avoid manufactured testimonials, and include a promotion only where permitted.

4. Prioritize opportunities for the responsible person and log the outcome. Move personal project details into the approved private lead process instead of exposing them in a public reply.

## Deliverable

Return a community directory, opportunity queue, rule notes, response drafts, and lead-handoff status.

## Quality checks

Do not impersonate a resident or customer, scrape restricted communities, or mass-post identical replies. Membership does not itself authorize publishing or direct messaging.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
