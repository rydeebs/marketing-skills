# Website content

## Purpose and inputs

Draft evidence-based case studies, use-case and solution pages, and landing pages for runwayml.com, and improve galleries, metadata, and internal links.

Runway (runwayml.com) builds generative video and media models and tools, sold through a self-serve web app, team and enterprise plans, and a developer API. Buyers range from individual creators and creative professionals to agencies, brand marketing teams, media and entertainment studios, gaming companies, and developers building on the API. Check current model names, features, plan tiers, pricing, usage terms, and content policy against runwayml.com, the Help Center, or internal docs before stating them. Do not rely on memory, because the lineup changes often.

Start with: Website URLs, page inventory, target personas and verticals, approved customer story packs, current product facts, and search, analytics, and conversion data if available. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Audit existing pages for outdated model names, features, and pricing, missing use cases, weak answers, broken links, and overlapping coverage. Prioritize changes tied to a real buyer question or conversion path.

2. Draft case studies around the customer’s challenge, workflow, Runway features used, and verified results. Add an accurate title, meta description, visual plan, and CTA (sign up, contact sales, or API docs).

3. For use-case, industry, and solution pages, include distinct evidence and workflows for each audience. Do not generate interchangeable pages for segments Runway does not serve well.

4. Prepare descriptive filenames, alt text based on visible content, and captions describing how visuals were made. Propose contextual links to product, pricing, docs, and contact pages, and check destinations before handoff.

## Deliverable

Deliver page drafts or scoped edits, a gallery update list, a metadata table, an internal-link checklist, and a prioritized refresh backlog.

## Quality checks

Avoid keyword stuffing, invented results, and unsupported ranking promises. Validate rendered layout and links if editing the site, and keep existing redirects and SEO metadata unless a change is justified. Never overstate model capabilities or show cherry-picked generations as typical output without saying so. Respect likeness, voice, and IP rights in any generated or customer media, follow Runway’s content policy and AI-disclosure norms, and keep NDA’d customer, studio, and partner names out of public material without written approval.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
