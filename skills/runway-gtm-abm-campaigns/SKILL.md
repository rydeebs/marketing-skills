# ABM campaigns

## Purpose and inputs

Plan account-based campaigns for named target accounts, coordinating personalized content, ads, outreach, and optional physical sends, with response tracking.

Runway (runwayml.com) builds generative video and media models and tools, sold through a self-serve web app, team and enterprise plans, and a developer API. Buyers range from individual creators and creative professionals to agencies, brand marketing teams, media and entertainment studios, gaming companies, and developers building on the API. Check current model names, features, plan tiers, pricing, usage terms, and content policy against runwayml.com, the Help Center, or internal docs before stating them. Do not rely on memory, because the lineup changes often.

Start with: Target account list and tiering, ICP definition, buying-committee roles, approved assets and customer stories, budget, ad and intent platforms, gifting or print vendor rules, and sales owner for each account. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Choose and tier accounts based on ICP fit, intent and product-usage signals, existing relationships, and sales capacity. Confirm each account has an owner and no conflicting active deal motion.

2. Map the buying committee (creative, marketing, production, innovation, procurement, legal) with sourced contacts. Deduplicate and honor opt-outs and regional privacy rules.

3. Build the play: a personalized angle per account (for example a spec generation or workflow tailored to the brand, with permission and brand-safety review), coordinated ads, outreach sequence, event invitation, or gift or print piece. Track each touch with account-level codes or UTMs.

4. Create a production checklist covering assets, approvals, spend, timing, and sales follow-up SLAs. Measure engagement, meetings, opportunities, and pipeline by account against a holdout or baseline where possible.

## Deliverable

Deliver an account plan per tier, buying-committee map, personalized asset briefs, channel schedule, tracking plan, and budget assumptions.

## Quality checks

ABM spend, gifts, and ads have real cost and can raise anti-bribery or gift-policy issues; check recipient gift policies and never call it free. Do not generate content using a target’s brand or talent without clearance. Launching, paying, or sending requires explicit authorization. Never overstate model capabilities or show cherry-picked generations as typical output without saying so. Respect likeness, voice, and IP rights in any generated or customer media, follow Runway’s content policy and AI-disclosure norms, and keep NDA’d customer, studio, and partner names out of public material without written approval.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
