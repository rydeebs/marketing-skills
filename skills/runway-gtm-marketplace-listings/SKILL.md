# Marketplace & directory listings

## Purpose and inputs

Maintain accurate Runway listings on software marketplaces, integration directories, AI tool directories, and API model catalogs, and compare what each actually contributes.

Runway (runwayml.com) builds generative video and media models and tools, sold through a self-serve web app, team and enterprise plans, and a developer API. Buyers range from individual creators and creative professionals to agencies, brand marketing teams, media and entertainment studios, gaming companies, and developers building on the API. Check current model names, features, plan tiers, pricing, usage terms, and content policy against runwayml.com, the Help Center, or internal docs before stating them. Do not rely on memory, because the lineup changes often.

Start with: Listing URLs (for example cloud marketplaces, G2 and Capterra profiles, AI tool directories, plugin and integration directories, API catalogs), canonical product facts, approved assets, and attributed signup or lead data. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Audit each listing for current models, features, pricing, screenshots, categories, links, and duplicates. Confirm who owns the listing before proposing edits.

2. Prepare updates with accurate descriptions, current visuals and demo videos, and fields adapted to each platform instead of copying one blurb everywhere.

3. Draft responses to questions and reviews according to each platform’s policies, and route pricing negotiation, procurement, and security questions to sales or the appropriate owner.

4. Reconcile listing-sourced traffic, signups, and marketplace transactions to CRM and billing records. Track paid placement fees separately from organic contribution.

## Deliverable

Deliver a listing audit, update queue, response drafts, correction log, and a source-by-source contribution summary.

## Quality checks

Do not buy placements, change marketplace pricing or private offers, or accept new terms as routine maintenance. Count each signup or deal once, and flag missing attribution instead of crediting every listing. Never overstate model capabilities or show cherry-picked generations as typical output without saying so. Respect likeness, voice, and IP rights in any generated or customer media, follow Runway’s content policy and AI-disclosure norms, and keep NDA’d customer, studio, and partner names out of public material without written approval.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
