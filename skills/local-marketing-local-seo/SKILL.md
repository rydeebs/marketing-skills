# Local SEO

## Purpose and inputs

Audit local listings, track location-specific search visibility, and research relevant directories and local backlink opportunities. Adapt to the user’s actual business; do not assume West Coast Deck is the client.

Start with: Canonical business name, address or service-area policy, phone, website, service geography, listing URLs, and any existing rank-tracking setup. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Establish a canonical business record. Compare relevant listings against it and distinguish real errors from legitimate formatting differences or service-area privacy rules.

2. Track a stable set of service queries with the same geographic points, device assumptions, and dates. Record the method so apparent rank changes are interpretable.

3. Research legitimate local and industry directories, chambers, partner resources, and professional associations. Rank opportunities by geographic relevance, audience fit, legitimacy, and effort.

4. Prepare corrections or submission drafts with the exact destination and proposed fields. Research locally relevant links from partners and real projects; avoid bulk link schemes and duplicate profile creation.

## Deliverable

Return a citation audit with existing URL, discrepancy, proposed correction, status, and verified result; add a rank baseline and prioritized opportunity list.

## Quality checks

Do not treat one search from one location as an overall ranking. Verify listing rules and ownership before updates, and keep paid directory fees outside an organic-only budget unless approved.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
