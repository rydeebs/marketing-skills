# HOA / Property Management outreach

## Purpose and inputs

Research HOA and property-management prospects and prepare relevant outreach around balcony, deck, and waterproofing services. Adapt to the user’s actual business; do not assume West Coast Deck is the client.

Start with: Target geography, property types, minimum project fit, verified service capabilities, approved educational assets, and CRM exclusions. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Identify organizations with a plausible service need using public business information. Record evidence of portfolio/location fit and the research source/date.

2. Find the responsible business role and verify contact details where possible. Distinguish a management company, the property, and an HOA so ownership is not assumed.

3. Prepare a concise personalized introduction tied to the property type and a relevant portfolio example. Offer an educational resource or discussion without claiming an unseen building is unsafe or noncompliant.

4. Maintain a follow-up queue with last contact, response, next action, and stop condition. Route inspection questions, compliance interpretations, and technical assessments to a qualified person.

## Deliverable

Return a sourced prospect list, fit notes, CRM-ready fields, personalized drafts, educational asset brief, and follow-up queue.

## Quality checks

Verify applicable requirements before making regulatory claims. Research and drafting do not authorize bulk outreach, and opt-outs end the sequence.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
