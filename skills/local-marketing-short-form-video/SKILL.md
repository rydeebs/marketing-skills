# Short-form video

## Purpose and inputs

Turn field footage into edit-ready short videos or editor briefs for Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts. Adapt to the user’s actual business; do not assume West Coast Deck is the client.

Start with: Raw footage, project facts, desired audience, brand templates, approved audio sources, target channels, and available editing tools. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Inventory clips by project, phase, orientation, audio quality, and useful moment. Preserve originals and identify the best proof shots with timestamps.

2. Choose one story per video: a transformation, an installation detail, a common question, or a practical lesson. Build the opening around what the viewer can actually see.

3. Create a timecoded cut list with opening, sequence, voiceover or captions, and end card. Verify technical statements with the field team and flag missing shots.

4. If editing tools are available, export a platform-appropriate version with readable captions and safe text placement. Otherwise deliver a complete handoff to the editor rather than claiming a video was rendered.

## Deliverable

Provide an asset inventory, timecoded edit brief, exact on-screen text, caption copy, thumbnail suggestion, and export or editor handoff status.

## Quality checks

Use licensed audio and approved footage. Check caption accuracy, misleading cuts, private details, and current channel specifications before scheduling.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
