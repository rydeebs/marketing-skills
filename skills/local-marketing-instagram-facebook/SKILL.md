# Instagram / Facebook

## Purpose and inputs

Build and maintain an Instagram and Facebook publishing calendar for real local-service projects, including captions and routine response drafts. Adapt to the user’s actual business; do not assume West Coast Deck is the client.

Start with: Brand voice, service area, approved image/video assets, project facts, account destinations, publishing cadence, and current comments or DMs. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Group usable assets by project and homeowner benefit. Match before and after photos from the same project and explain the actual change without exaggeration.

2. Prepare a balanced calendar of finished work, process, useful answers, and customer proof. Choose a feed post, Story, or Reel because it fits the asset and message.

3. Write platform-specific captions with a concrete opening, verified project detail, and a simple call to action. Add location/product tags only when accurate and appropriate.

4. Draft answers to routine comments and inquiries. Capture qualified leads in the existing lead process; send pricing commitments, technical disputes, and complaints to the responsible human.

## Deliverable

Deliver a calendar containing platform, date, format, asset links, caption, tags, CTA, and publication state; provide a response queue with priority and owner.

## Quality checks

Check asset rights, mobile crops, legibility, and destination links. Report actual published URLs and attributable inquiries when available, not just likes.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
