# Houzz / Yelp / Angi etc.

## Purpose and inputs

Maintain accurate profiles and portfolios on home-service discovery platforms and compare their actual lead contribution. Adapt to the user’s actual business; do not assume West Coast Deck is the client.

Start with: Platform/profile URLs, canonical business facts, approved project packs, message/review history, lead records, and subscription terms if relevant. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Audit each existing profile for service information, coverage area, contact paths, credentials, gallery freshness, and duplicates. Confirm platform ownership before proposing edits.

2. Prepare project uploads with accurate scope, city, descriptions, and approved images. Adapt fields to each platform instead of blindly copying the same material.

3. Draft timely responses to inquiries and reviews. Follow each platform’s current policies, especially review solicitation rules, and route complaints or binding quote requests to the business owner.

4. Reconcile platform inquiries to CRM records and appointments. Record fees separately so organic discovery is distinguishable from paid lead products.

## Deliverable

Deliver a platform audit, project upload queue, response drafts, correction log, and source-by-source lead/appointment summary.

## Quality checks

Do not enroll in paid lead programs or change subscriptions as routine profile maintenance. Count duplicates once and identify missing source data instead of crediting every platform.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
