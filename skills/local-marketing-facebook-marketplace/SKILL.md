# Facebook Marketplace

## Purpose and inputs

Assess Facebook Marketplace eligibility and manage permitted listings or alternative channel drafts for a local service business. Adapt to the user’s actual business; do not assume West Coast Deck is the client.

Start with: Offering details, account access, service area, approved images, inquiry process, and current official Marketplace/Commerce policies. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. First check current official rules for the exact offering and account. Do not assume decks, installation, waterproofing, or other services qualify for Marketplace listings.

2. If the offering is prohibited or eligibility is unclear, stop listing creation and prepare content for an allowed business-page or other suitable channel; explain the limitation.

3. For a permitted offering, audit existing listings for accuracy, condition, price, geography, availability, and asset rights. Draft truthful copy without disguising a service as a physical product.

4. Plan updates within platform rules and route inquiries into the existing lead workflow. Track qualified inquiries and appointments by listing rather than relying on view counts.

## Deliverable

Deliver an eligibility finding with official source and checked date, then either compliant listing drafts/update notes or an alternative-channel content pack.

## Quality checks

Do not use fake prices, duplicate-listing churn, or category workarounds. Never promise a listing is live unless its publication is independently verified.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
