# Lifecycle email

## Purpose and inputs

Build segmented lifecycle and campaign email for Runway users and prospects, covering onboarding, activation, upgrades, launches, and newsletters, with measurable outcomes.

Runway (runwayml.com) builds generative video and media models and tools, sold through a self-serve web app, team and enterprise plans, and a developer API. Buyers range from individual creators and creative professionals to agencies, brand marketing teams, media and entertainment studios, gaming companies, and developers building on the API. Check current model names, features, plan tiers, pricing, usage terms, and content policy against runwayml.com, the Help Center, or internal docs before stating them. Do not rely on memory, because the lineup changes often.

Start with: Permissioned audience export, suppression list, product-usage events, plan data, campaign history, brand voice, launch calendar, offer facts, and ESP or marketing-automation access. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Normalize and deduplicate the audience while preserving consent, source, and suppression history. Segment by plan, lifecycle stage, usage and activation signals, persona, and region.

2. Choose one primary goal per campaign, such as activation, upgrade, feature adoption, launch awareness, or event registration, and an eligible audience. Use real generations, tutorials, and customer proof.

3. Write subject line options, preheader, body, CTA, and plain-text version. Use valid personalization fallbacks, verify model names, plan features, pricing and offer terms, and confirm unsubscribe handling.

4. Prepare audience counts, exclusions, tracking links, holdouts where useful, and a test checklist. After an authorized send, reconcile clicks, activations, upgrades, pipeline, and unsubscribes, and treat opens as an imperfect signal.

## Deliverable

Deliver campaign copy, segment rules and counts, suppression summary, test results, and a results table with denominators and attribution limits.

## Quality checks

Do not email scraped or unpermissioned contacts, and follow regional consent rules. Scheduling or sending requires current authorization and a confirmed account, audience, and content. Never overstate model capabilities or show cherry-picked generations as typical output without saying so. Respect likeness, voice, and IP rights in any generated or customer media, follow Runway’s content policy and AI-disclosure norms, and keep NDA’d customer, studio, and partner names out of public material without written approval.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
