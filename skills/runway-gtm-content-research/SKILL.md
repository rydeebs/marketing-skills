# Content research

## Purpose and inputs

Turn real creator, buyer, and developer questions about Runway and AI video into a prioritized content backlog and ready-to-produce briefs.

Runway (runwayml.com) builds generative video and media models and tools, sold through a self-serve web app, team and enterprise plans, and a developer API. Buyers range from individual creators and creative professionals to agencies, brand marketing teams, media and entertainment studios, gaming companies, and developers building on the API. Check current model names, features, plan tiers, pricing, usage terms, and content policy against runwayml.com, the Help Center, or internal docs before stating them. Do not rely on memory, because the lineup changes often.

Start with: Target audiences (creators, filmmakers, marketers, agencies, developers), current content inventory, sales and support questions, community threads, and access to search, Reddit, YouTube, and social sources. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Collect questions from sales calls, support tickets, community threads, search, Reddit, and YouTube. Save sources and dates, and remove identifying customer details.

2. Cluster questions by audience, job to be done, and buying stage: awareness (what can AI video do), evaluation (Runway versus alternatives, pricing, rights), adoption (workflows, prompting, API integration), and expansion.

3. Prioritize topics by relevance, repeated evidence, Runway expertise, and gaps in existing content. Do not fabricate search volumes or call a question common based on one example.

4. Prepare briefs with the exact question, a strong opening, answer outline, generations or workflows to show, proof needed, format (tutorial, guide, livestream, short), and a modest CTA. Flag technical claims for product review.

## Deliverable

Return a sourced question bank, ranked editorial backlog, and production-ready briefs with audience, evidence, owner, and channel.

## Quality checks

Write original answers instead of copying sources. Mark uncertainty and avoid claims about model behavior, rights, or pricing that product and legal have not confirmed. Never overstate model capabilities or show cherry-picked generations as typical output without saying so. Respect likeness, voice, and IP rights in any generated or customer media, follow Runway’s content policy and AI-disclosure norms, and keep NDA’d customer, studio, and partner names out of public material without written approval.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
