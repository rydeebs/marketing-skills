# Agency & studio partners

## Purpose and inputs

Build relationships with creative agencies, production companies, and post houses that can adopt Runway, co-sell it, or deliver Runway-powered work for clients.

Runway (runwayml.com) builds generative video and media models and tools, sold through a self-serve web app, team and enterprise plans, and a developer API. Buyers range from individual creators and creative professionals to agencies, brand marketing teams, media and entertainment studios, gaming companies, and developers building on the API. Check current model names, features, plan tiers, pricing, usage terms, and content policy against runwayml.com, the Help Center, or internal docs before stating them. Do not rely on memory, because the lineup changes often.

Start with: Target segments (advertising agencies, production companies, VFX/post houses, creative studios), regions, existing partner and customer records, approved showcase work, and current partner program terms if any. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Research agencies and studios whose public work, client roster, or stated AI practice shows a genuine fit. Capture one specific project, reel, or statement that establishes relevance, with source and date.

2. Identify the right role (head of innovation, ECD, creative technologist, head of production) and record source, contact confidence, and existing relationship history in the CRM. Avoid redundant contacts across the same holding company or studio group.

3. Select one Runway showcase piece or customer story that matches the firm’s work. Draft a short introduction covering what the team could produce faster or differently, how onboarding and support work, and a modest next step such as a workflow session or pilot.

4. Engage thoughtfully on the firm’s public work only where there is something useful to add. Track replies, meetings, pilots, seats activated, and client projects instead of rewarding raw activity.

## Deliverable

Deliver a prioritized agency/studio list, fit evidence, showcase match, outreach drafts, and a partner tracker with stage, owner, and next action.

## Quality checks

Do not pretend to know a creative lead or praise work you have not inspected. Do not promise partner discounts, co-marketing, revenue share, or roadmap features without approved terms. Never overstate model capabilities or show cherry-picked generations as typical output without saying so. Respect likeness, voice, and IP rights in any generated or customer media, follow Runway’s content policy and AI-disclosure norms, and keep NDA’d customer, studio, and partner names out of public material without written approval.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
