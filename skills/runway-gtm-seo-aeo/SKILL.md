# SEO & AI answer visibility

## Purpose and inputs

Track Runway’s visibility in search and AI answer engines for target queries, and find legitimate ways to improve it through content, citations, and authoritative links.

Runway (runwayml.com) builds generative video and media models and tools, sold through a self-serve web app, team and enterprise plans, and a developer API. Buyers range from individual creators and creative professionals to agencies, brand marketing teams, media and entertainment studios, gaming companies, and developers building on the API. Check current model names, features, plan tiers, pricing, usage terms, and content policy against runwayml.com, the Help Center, or internal docs before stating them. Do not rely on memory, because the lineup changes often.

Start with: Priority query set (category, use-case, comparison, and how-to queries), site analytics and Search Console access, existing rank-tracking setup, target AI assistants to sample, and current content inventory. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Define a stable query set by intent and persona. Track rankings with consistent location, device, and dates, and record the method so changes can be interpreted.

2. Sample AI answer engines and assistants for the same queries on a fixed schedule. Record whether Runway is mentioned or cited, which sources are cited, and whether product facts are accurate.

3. Identify gaps: missing or outdated pages, weak answers, inaccurate third-party descriptions, and authoritative sites such as publications, directories, and educational resources that cover the category but omit Runway.

4. Prepare fixes: content updates, structured data, correction requests to third parties, and outreach for earned mentions tied to real resources. Avoid link schemes and mass-produced pages.

## Deliverable

Return a search and AI-visibility baseline, gap analysis, citation and correction list, and a prioritized opportunity backlog.

## Quality checks

Do not treat one query sample or one assistant’s answer as overall visibility. Keep paid placements outside organic reporting, and never promise rankings or AI citations. Never overstate model capabilities or show cherry-picked generations as typical output without saying so. Respect likeness, voice, and IP rights in any generated or customer media, follow Runway’s content policy and AI-disclosure norms, and keep NDA’d customer, studio, and partner names out of public material without written approval.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
