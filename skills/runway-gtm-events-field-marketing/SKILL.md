# Events & field marketing

## Purpose and inputs

Plan Runway’s presence at conferences, festivals, screenings, meetups, and workshops, including booth and demo logistics, meeting booking, and lead capture.

Runway (runwayml.com) builds generative video and media models and tools, sold through a self-serve web app, team and enterprise plans, and a developer API. Buyers range from individual creators and creative professionals to agencies, brand marketing teams, media and entertainment studios, gaming companies, and developers building on the API. Check current model names, features, plan tiers, pricing, usage terms, and content policy against runwayml.com, the Help Center, or internal docs before stating them. Do not rely on memory, because the lineup changes often.

Start with: Event calendar (for example industry trade shows, film festivals, ad festivals, graphics conferences, and Runway’s own film festival or community events, verified per year), goals, budget, staffing, demo setup, and the lead-capture tool. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Build an event roster with dates, audience, goal (pipeline, brand, community, recruiting), cost, owner, and a go/no-go rationale. Confirm current event details and deadlines from official sources.

2. Plan pre-event outreach: target account lists, meeting booking, speaker or screening submissions, and invitations to side events. Coordinate with sales on account ownership.

3. Prepare an on-site checklist: demo stations and fallbacks for offline or slow networks, approved showcase reels, collateral, badge-scan or form capture with consent, staffing schedule, and proof photos.

4. Run post-event follow-up within the agreed SLA, with segmented messages by conversation type, and track meetings, opportunities, and pipeline sourced or influenced by the event.

## Deliverable

Deliver an event roster, per-event plan, material and staffing needs, lead-capture and follow-up plan, and a results summary.

## Quality checks

A remote agent cannot confirm a booth, screening, or asset is physically in place without on-site evidence. Sponsorships, contracts, and travel spend require explicit authorization. Never overstate model capabilities or show cherry-picked generations as typical output without saying so. Respect likeness, voice, and IP rights in any generated or customer media, follow Runway’s content policy and AI-disclosure norms, and keep NDA’d customer, studio, and partner names out of public material without written approval.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
