# Email marketing

## Purpose and inputs

Prepare segmented newsletters and seasonal service email campaigns with contact hygiene and measurable lead tracking. Adapt to the user’s actual business; do not assume West Coast Deck is the client.

Start with: Permissioned contact export, suppression list, campaign history, brand voice, service calendar, offer facts, and email platform access if available. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Normalize and deduplicate the list while preserving consent, source, and suppression history. Segment by customer/prospect status, relevant service, geography, and lifecycle stage.

2. Choose one primary campaign goal and an eligible audience. Draft a monthly newsletter or seasonal maintenance message using real project proof and a clear next step.

3. Create subject line options, preheader, body, CTA, and a plain-text version. Use valid personalization fallbacks and verify offer terms, contact details, and unsubscribe handling.

4. Prepare audience counts, exclusions, tracking links, and a test checklist. After an authorized send, reconcile clicks, replies, qualified inquiries, bookings, and unsubscribes; treat opens as an imperfect signal.

## Deliverable

Deliver campaign copy, segment rules and counts, suppression summary, test results, and a reporting table with denominators and attribution limits.

## Quality checks

Do not send to scraped or unpermissioned contacts as a newsletter audience. Scheduling or sending requires the user’s current authorization and confirmed account, audience, and content.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
