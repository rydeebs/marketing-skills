# Short-form video

## Purpose and inputs

Turn Runway generations, product workflows, and creator work into edit-ready short videos or editor briefs for Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and X.

Runway (runwayml.com) builds generative video and media models and tools, sold through a self-serve web app, team and enterprise plans, and a developer API. Buyers range from individual creators and creative professionals to agencies, brand marketing teams, media and entertainment studios, gaming companies, and developers building on the API. Check current model names, features, plan tiers, pricing, usage terms, and content policy against runwayml.com, the Help Center, or internal docs before stating them. Do not rely on memory, because the lineup changes often.

Start with: Raw generations and screen recordings, prompts and settings used, creator permissions, audience, brand templates, licensed audio, target channels, and available editing tools. Use supplied context first. Ask only for missing facts that block a correct result; mark other gaps and continue.

## Workflow

1. Inventory clips by model or feature, theme, orientation, quality, and standout moments. Keep originals and prompt metadata, and mark the best shots with timestamps.

2. Choose one story per video, such as a capability reveal, a prompt-to-output walkthrough, a before-and-after workflow, a creator spotlight, or a practical tip. Open on what the viewer can actually see.

3. Create a timecoded cut list with hook, sequence, voiceover or captions, on-screen prompts, and end card. Confirm technical statements with product owners and flag missing shots.

4. If editing tools are available, export channel-appropriate versions with readable captions and safe text placement. Otherwise hand off a complete brief to the editor instead of claiming a video was rendered.

## Deliverable

Provide an asset inventory, timecoded edit brief, exact on-screen text, caption copy, thumbnail idea, and export or handoff status.

## Quality checks

Use licensed audio and approved footage, credit creators, and be honest about how many tries a result took and how much human editing was involved. Check caption accuracy and current channel specs before scheduling. Never overstate model capabilities or show cherry-picked generations as typical output without saying so. Respect likeness, voice, and IP rights in any generated or customer media, follow Runway’s content policy and AI-disclosure norms, and keep NDA’d customer, studio, and partner names out of public material without written approval.

## Execution boundary

Use the tools available in the working environment; if access is missing, deliver a usable draft or handoff and state the limitation. Follow the user’s existing authorization and tool policies. Before any external change or communication, confirm the account, destination, and scope; this skill itself grants no authority to publish, send, purchase, or delete. Record confirmed results and stop after at most two retries of a failed write unless the service proves it did not land.
