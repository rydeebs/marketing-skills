# Marketing Skills

Claude skills for local service business marketing. Each folder in `skills/` contains a `SKILL.md`.

## Install

```bash
cp -R skills/* ~/.claude/skills/
```

## Skills

- [`local-marketing-architect-designer-outreach`](skills/local-marketing-architect-designer-outreach/SKILL.md) — Build relationships with relevant architects and designers through sourced research and tailored portfolio introductions.
- [`local-marketing-builder-gc-relationships`](skills/local-marketing-builder-gc-relationships/SKILL.md) — Research builders and general contractors and prepare a subcontractor or specialist relationship pipeline.
- [`local-marketing-community-groups`](skills/local-marketing-community-groups/SKILL.md) — Monitor relevant community groups for homeowner questions and prepare helpful, rule-compliant responses and opportunity handoffs.
- [`local-marketing-competitive-research`](skills/local-marketing-competitive-research/SKILL.md) — Compare local competitors using public evidence and produce testable marketing opportunities without inventing performance estimates.
- [`local-marketing-content-research`](skills/local-marketing-content-research/SKILL.md) — Turn real homeowner questions into a prioritized content backlog and practical on-camera briefs.
- [`local-marketing-crm-hygiene`](skills/local-marketing-crm-hygiene/SKILL.md) — Audit and clean marketing CRM records while preserving attribution, contact preferences, and relationship history.
- [`local-marketing-customer-reviews`](skills/local-marketing-customer-reviews/SKILL.md) — Run a post-project review workflow with neutral requests, follow-up tracking, response drafts, and customer issue escalation.
- [`local-marketing-direct-mail`](skills/local-marketing-direct-mail/SKILL.md) — Plan geographically targeted direct-mail campaigns around real projects, including mailing lists, postcard briefs, and response tracking.
- [`local-marketing-directory-platforms`](skills/local-marketing-directory-platforms/SKILL.md) — Maintain accurate profiles and portfolios on home-service discovery platforms and compare their actual lead contribution.
- [`local-marketing-email-marketing`](skills/local-marketing-email-marketing/SKILL.md) — Prepare segmented newsletters and seasonal service email campaigns with contact hygiene and measurable lead tracking.
- [`local-marketing-facebook-marketplace`](skills/local-marketing-facebook-marketplace/SKILL.md) — Assess Facebook Marketplace eligibility and manage permitted listings or alternative channel drafts for a local service business.
- [`local-marketing-google-business-profile`](skills/local-marketing-google-business-profile/SKILL.md) — Maintain a local service business Google Business Profile and prepare project posts, photo updates, factual answers, and review responses.
- [`local-marketing-hoa-property-management`](skills/local-marketing-hoa-property-management/SKILL.md) — Research HOA and property-management prospects and prepare relevant outreach around balcony, deck, and waterproofing services.
- [`local-marketing-instagram-facebook`](skills/local-marketing-instagram-facebook/SKILL.md) — Build and maintain an Instagram and Facebook publishing calendar for real local-service projects, including captions and routine response drafts.
- [`local-marketing-jobsite-marketing`](skills/local-marketing-jobsite-marketing/SKILL.md) — Coordinate signs, banners, and nearby marketing opportunities around active home-service jobs.
- [`local-marketing-local-seo`](skills/local-marketing-local-seo/SKILL.md) — Audit local listings, track location-specific search visibility, and research relevant directories and local backlink opportunities.
- [`local-marketing-marketing-reporting`](skills/local-marketing-marketing-reporting/SKILL.md) — Build a weekly local-service marketing report with reconciled lead sources, activity, appointments, and explicit attribution limits.
- [`local-marketing-past-customer-marketing`](skills/local-marketing-past-customer-marketing/SKILL.md) — Find appropriate maintenance, repeat-service, and referral opportunities in past-customer records and prepare personal check-ins.
- [`local-marketing-pr-publications`](skills/local-marketing-pr-publications/SKILL.md) — Develop local media, home/design publication, and project award opportunities with tailored pitches and submission packages.
- [`local-marketing-project-content`](skills/local-marketing-project-content/SKILL.md) — Organize a completed construction or home-service project into a verified source pack for reusable marketing content.
- [`local-marketing-referral-program`](skills/local-marketing-referral-program/SKILL.md) — Maintain referral partner relationships, lead handoffs, and follow-up records for a local service business.
- [`local-marketing-short-form-video`](skills/local-marketing-short-form-video/SKILL.md) — Turn field footage into edit-ready short videos or editor briefs for Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.
- [`local-marketing-website-seo`](skills/local-marketing-website-seo/SKILL.md) — Draft evidence-based project case studies and useful local service pages, and improve galleries, image metadata, and internal links.
